Dear Editors of Strain,

Please consider the manuscript "Ray-field residuals as a diagnostic for compact stereo-microscope calibration".

Stereo microscope calibration requires a choice of geometric constraints, and a low fitting residual does not establish that optical parameters are independently identified. This manuscript uses an existing fitted ray field to examine these constraints through comparable line residuals, spatial decomposition and parameter-invariance checks.

The case study shows that independent arm transformations remove a dominant constant directional mismatch, while a freely fitted central comparator improves further on the original shared model. Controlled numerical perturbations test when a rigid correction is sufficient and when additional spatial terms are useful. The article presents a reproducible calibration diagnostic relevant to stereo DIC; it does not claim a new experimental strain validation or unique recovery of the microscope's optical architecture.

The main article is accompanied by executable analysis, numerical outputs and supplementary methodological details. All new computations use existing data or explicitly labelled synthetic rays.

Jean-François Witz
