Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Rayfield-Based Calibration and Physical Model
Identification of a Common Main Objective Stereo Microscope" for
consideration in Optics Express.

This paper presents the first real-data validation of a non-central
stereo calibration pipeline on a Common Main Objective (CMO) stereo
microscope. The key contribution is methodological: we show that a
per-pixel Zernike rayfield, fitted to ChArUco corner observations,
serves as a diagnostic instrument that reveals which physical degree
of freedom a candidate model is missing. Residual-guided hypothesis
construction leads from a naive perspective model (86 px RMS) through
a telecentric CMO skeleton (14.6 px) to a 26-parameter CMO+SE(3)
model achieving 1.06 px RMS on real Pycaso calibration images, where
OpenCV stereo calibration fails (>300 px).

The work is supported by:
- Extensive residual analysis (Zernike modal decomposition of direction
  and moment differences),
- Formal BIC model selection with an operational reprojection guard,
- Internal validation (cross-validation, bootstrap, fx sensitivity),
- Full reproducibility (code, data, and intermediate state artefacts).

We believe this work will interest the Optics Express readership because
it demonstrates a generalisable strategy---measure first, model second,
diagnose by residual---applicable to any non-standard optical instrument.

All authors have approved the manuscript. It is not under consideration
elsewhere.
