(line 668, `zernike_cmo_rigid_removed.pdf`).

  percentile parameters.

## Known issue (faithfully reproduced)

The published `zernike_cmo_rigid_removed.pdf` was generated by an early
helper (`convert_figures_to_pdf.py`) that applied the Kabsch SE(3) — fit
on 3-D world points in millimetres — to **pixel-coordinate triples
`(j, i, Z)`** instead of the 3-D `(X, Y, Z)` points. That mixes pixel
indices with millimetres in the rotation, which is dimensionally wrong:
the `dZ raw` panel reports `med ≈ 367 mm` (i.e. several times the
working distance), an obvious tell of the bug. The two plane-normalised
panels are still meaningful because the affine plane absorbs the global
ramp introduced by the bad SE(3) application.

This generator **faithfully reproduces the buggy PDF** so manuscript
regeneration is bit-equivalent. The numerically correct alternative is
already produced as a PNG by
`examples/notebooks/11_compare_zernike_cmo_rigid_removed.py`
(`zernike_cmo_rigid_removed.png`, applies the SE(3) to 3-D mm points,
`dZ raw` then reports `med ≈ 0.06 mm` as expected).

When the manuscript is next revised, the LaTeX `\includegraphics` should
be repointed to that PNG and this generator deleted — or fixed in
place. Until then, the two files coexist with different content and the
PDF is the canonical reference.
