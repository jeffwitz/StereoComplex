# Figure — External profilometry validation of the specimen relief

Pycaso-Fig.9-style comparison of the embossed letter **"E"** relief on the coin
specimen: a profilometric scan (external 3-D ground truth) versus three
stereo-reconstruction models sharing one dense DIS correspondence field.

- **Produced figure:** `profilo_relief_comparison.pdf` (vector, manuscript) and
  `profilo_relief_comparison.png` (docs/preview sibling).
- **Referenced in the manuscript:** external-validation discussion of the
  specimen section (CMO paper). Figure number assigned at integration time.
- **Generator:** `examples/notebooks/generate_fig_profilo_relief_comparison.py`.

## The narrative it establishes

| Surface | relief std | vs profilo | cc vs profilo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profilometry (ground truth) | 20.8 µm | 1.00× | — |
| Soloff (deg 3) | 22.8 µm | 1.09× | 0.958 |
| **CMO 26p (this work)** | 23.0 µm | 1.10× | 0.958 |
| Zernike rayfield (stage-anchored) | 21.6 µm | 1.04× | 0.958 |

**Soloff and CMO 26p are indistinguishable** on the relief:
`cc = 0.9997`, `RMS difference = 0.57 µm`. The stage-anchored Zernike map is
also nearly identical to Soloff (`cc > 0.99999`, `RMS = 1.21 µm`) and has the
closest amplitude to the profilometry (`1.04×`). The free-pose `1.63×`
over-amplification diagnosed in Figure 10 therefore comes from the missing
stage-metric pose prior, not from the Zernike basis.

## Editable inputs (this folder)

- `coin_profilo_recale.npy` — profilometric scan of the "E", pre-registered to
  the left-camera pixel scale by Eddy (800×700, µm). The external ground truth.
- `dis_params.json` — DIS optical-flow configuration (full image, variational
  refinement `alpha=120, epsilon=1e-4, 50 iterations`). Includes the tuning
  trace (`cc` 0.865 → 0.958) from the DISFlow→figure→DISFlow loop.
- `registration.json` — rigid (similarity, scale≈1.03) transform mapping the
  profilo onto the 2048² left-image canvas, derived once by letter-mask IoU
  (0.88). Flow-independent (aligns the "E" geometry).
- `relief_comparison_data.npz` — **cache**: the four windowed, plane-normalised
  reliefs (profilometry / Soloff / CMO 26p / stage-anchored Zernike) the figure
  draws from. Regenerated by `--recompute`.
- `dis_robustness.json` — **cache** of the DIS-robustness sweep: the per-setting
  amplitude ratios and CMO–Soloff agreement under the tuned parameters and the
  bare OpenCV ULTRAFAST/FAST/MEDIUM preset defaults. Feeds the LaTeX table
  `paper/cmo/tables/dis_robustness.tex` (manuscript `tab:dis_robustness`).
  Regenerated by `generate_tab_dis_robustness.py --recompute`.

## DIS-robustness table (answers the "tuned-on-the-ground-truth" objection)

The production DIS parameters were tuned by inspecting the profilometric
agreement. `examples/notebooks/generate_tab_dis_robustness.py` re-runs the
original free-pose diagnostic under **non-tuned** preset defaults and shows
that its `1.59–1.67×` gain is invariant to DIS. Figure 10 then changes the pose
prior while holding the tuned field fixed, and this figure displays the
selected stage-anchored result.

Calibration inputs are read from `docs/assets/pycaso_real_data/`
(`intermediate_state.npz`) and
`docs/assets/cmo_paper/figure10b_zernike_stage_prior/`
(`near_hard_zernike_rayfield.npz`).

## Regenerate

```bash
# fast: redraw from the versioned cache (no heavy data needed)
rtk .venv/bin/python examples/notebooks/generate_fig_profilo_relief_comparison.py

# full: re-run DIS flow + reconstruction from the raw Pycaso coin images,
# rewrite the cache, then draw. Needs the git-ignored examples/pycaso_data/ tree.
rtk .venv/bin/python examples/notebooks/evaluate_zernike_stage_prior.py
rtk .venv/bin/python examples/notebooks/generate_fig_profilo_relief_comparison.py --recompute
```

The raw `coin.tif` images live under `examples/pycaso_data/` (git-ignored
Pycaso example dataset), exactly like the cached reconstructions behind the
other specimen figures. The cache here is the versioned, reproducible unit.
